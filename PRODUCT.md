# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cybersecurity learners in Azerbaijan (and globally), primarily students and early-career practitioners. They come with a task: complete a mission, continue a course, climb the leaderboard. They are in-flow; the interface should disappear into the work.

## Product Purpose

Xakker is a Azerbaijani-first cybersecurity learning platform. Missions, labs, courses, and learning paths guide users from recruit to expert. Success means users can log in, find their next challenge immediately, and track visible progress (XP, rank, streak).

## Brand Personality

Sharp, technical, earned. Not edgy-for-edgy-sake — earned credibility. Like a well-maintained terminal: everything is where you expect it, nothing wastes space, and the red accent means danger (or achievement).

## Anti-references

- Gamified-bright consumer apps (Duolingo-style candy colors, excessive bouncy animations)
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards (navy + teal, rounded everything)
- Hacker-aesthetic clichés (matrix rain, neon green, skull icons)

## Design Principles

1. **Tool first.** The interface disappears into the task. Navigation is instant and predictable.
2. **Earned density.** Information is dense where density helps (leaderboards, stats) and spacious where focus matters (mission content).
3. **One accent, used precisely.** #ff3b3b marks action, achievement, and danger — nothing else.
4. **Bilingual by default.** AZ/EN parity is a structural requirement, not a feature toggle.
5. **State is visible.** Progress, streak, rank, and XP are always present. Users should never wonder where they stand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced-motion support required on all animations. AZ and EN content parity.
